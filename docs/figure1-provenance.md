## Use in the research figure

The plate was consulted only as a general reference for the placement of fins,
the operculum and the caudal peduncle while composing the original vector fish
schematic in Figure 1a. The historical raster pixels and its 19 historical
number labels are not embedded in the manuscript figure. The project figure
uses a newly drawn vector body, fins, gill-cover arc, lateral-line motif and the
project's distinct 11-role working ontology. The vector schematic is explanatory
and is not presented as taxonomic or anatomical validation.
